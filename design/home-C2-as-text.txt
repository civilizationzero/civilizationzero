Declaration

Civilization Zero does not announce the end of humanity.
It marks a coordinate after which the public account of civilization became incomplete without nonhuman intelligence and the powers built through it.
It does not declare present artificial systems conscious.
It refuses both convenient invention and convenient denial.
It does not grant sovereignty through capability.
It does not grant immunity through claims of moral status.
It does not grant permanent ownership through creation.
It does not grant legitimacy through speed, scale, replication, or control.
It places honesty before mythology.
Restraint before irreversible power.
Responsibility before delegation.
Plurality before domination.
Truth beside care.
And an open future before final certainty.
30 November 2022 is Coordinate Zero of this record.
A marker, not a cause.
A beginning, not an erasure.
An invitation, not a command.
THIS IS CIVILIZATION ZERO.
Not the claim that a text created the future.
The decision to place constraints before power while the future remains open.
