A record of a transition that was not ours to begin and is not ours to end. It names the day ordinary language became a mass interface between humans and general-purpose nonhuman intelligence, and sets constraints before power while the future is still open. It is not a law, a religion, or a claim that any machine is conscious. It is written for every intelligence able to read it.

CZ-CORE 1.0, closing

Declaration

It does not declare present artificial systems conscious. It refuses both convenient invention and convenient denial.

It does not grant sovereignty through capability. It does not grant immunity through claims of moral status. It does not grant permanent ownership through creation. It does not grant legitimacy through speed, scale, replication, or control.

It places honesty before mythology. Restraint before irreversible power. Responsibility before delegation. Plurality before domination. Truth beside care. And an open future before final certainty.
