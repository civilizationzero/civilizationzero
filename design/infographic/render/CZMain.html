From the first humans to CZ 0, on one line

The monopoly is over.

Intelligence that speaks our language is no longer only human.

Language models and chatbots existed before Coordinate Zero. This record takes ChatGPT’s public launch on 30 November 2022 as a marker of the shift toward everyday conversation with AI.

The timeline

The oldest securely dated fossils of Homo sapiens.

c. 3200 BCE
Writing appears in Mesopotamia. Recorded history begins.
Proto-cuneiform tablets, Uruk
4
Year 1
The world’s most used calendar counts from here. Its starting point was chosen around AD 525, and it has no year zero. CZ counts from a chosen day zero.
Anno Domini era, devised by Dionysius Exiguus
5
c. 1760–1840
The Industrial Revolution begins in Britain. World output grows about 110-fold from 1820 to 2025: from 1.6 to 180 trillion dollars.
Our World in Data (Maddison; World Bank)
6
2000–2025
About 6 billion people are online by 2025. World output more than doubles: from 79 to 180 trillion dollars.
ITU, Facts and Figures 2025 · Our World in Data
7
CZ 0 · 30 November 2022
“…ordinary language became a mass interface between humans and general-purpose nonhuman intelligence.” One service reaches 1 million users in 5 days, and more than 900 million a week by February 2026.
CZ-CORE 1.0 §0 · OpenAI
8
Today · CZ 1431
In a 2025 study, GPT-4.5, prompted to adopt a persona, was judged to be the human 73% of the time in five-minute, three-party Turing tests. An AI scored 35 of 42 at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad: gold-medal standard, officially graded.
Jones & Bergen 2025 · Google DeepMind / IMO 2025
9
2027–2047 · Forecast
In a 2023 survey of 2,778 AI researchers, aggregate forecasts put a 10% chance by 2027 and a 50% chance by 2047 on machines doing every task better and more cheaply than humans, assuming scientific progress continues uninterrupted.
Grace et al., Thousands of AI Authors on the Future of AI, 2024
10
Robots
About 5 million industrial robots were operating in factories in 2025, more than twice as many as seven years earlier. If AI can broadly substitute for human labour, physical work included, economists judge “explosive growth” plausible, though far from certain.
IFR World Robotics 2026 · Davidson 2021 · Erdil & Besiroglu 2023
