Archive, signature, mirrors

What is kept, how it can be checked, and what is still pending — with dates. Promises here are marked as what they are: a commitment, an implementation, or a verified result; they are not the same thing.

Identity is the hash

The record's identity is the SHA-256 of its canonical text, printed on /core, in the manifest, in llms.txt and in every bundle. An address may fail; the hash does not. The hash is of the exact bytes — UTF-8, no byte-order mark, LF line endings, nothing normalised. A copy that re-encodes line endings would otherwise look altered.

Signature and timestamp

Pending. A hash served by the same domain proves consistency, not that the operator changed nothing. The record will carry a public key (Ed25519), generated on the originating human's device and held by two people, its fingerprint printed in the Core's integrity record, and a detached signature over the manifest and each bundle; the manifest hash will be timestamped with OpenTimestamps and the proof kept in the repository and the bundle, with the block height and transaction once confirmed. The bundle

/archive/cz-bundle-1.0-pre.tar: the canonical texts, their JSON forms, the blueprint, the objections register, every review archived so far, the licences, the manifest, the machine prompt, and a README stating the encoding and hashing conventions. One file; no dependency on HTTP. The signed bundle for 1.0 replaces this pre-release bundle at launch; this one stays at its address.

Verification, succession, correction

  • Daily verification — a job that fetches the live bytes, recomputes the published hashes and alerts on drift; its results published here. Pending — target 2026-11-30.
  • Build-time verification — every quotation on every page is compared byte-for-byte with the canonical text; the JSON forms are reconstructed into the canonical bytes; the build fails on any drift. Implemented (site/check.py in the repository).
  • Succession — a named second key-holder outside the originator's company and jurisdiction; a published procedure for transfer of domain, repository and accounts; prepaid renewal of the domain for its longest term; a dead-man's procedure (if the administrator does not confirm within 90 days of a scheduled check, the second key-holder takes custody). Pending — the second key-holder is not yet named.
  • Recovery exercise — once a year, the site restored from the bundle on a host the originator does not control, with the result published. First: 2027.
  • Correction before 2031 — objections and errata are filed in the register by anyone; the originator responds within 60 days; a consequential error may produce an erratum note published beside the text without changing 1.0. A successor version is a new file with its own hash, change log, contributors, reasons, evidence and unresolved objections (IR 10.4); 1.0 stays at its addresses.
  • Change history — the repository's commits.
