Civilization Zero in images

Sixteen places, one for each ring of the day's figure, standing here in the order of their rings, the innermost first. A place is held by one sentence, posted as a short film; its last frame stands here.

How a sentence holds a place

An open place is taken by the next sentence posted as a film. When all sixteen are held, a newer sentence takes a place only if it has more views than the least viewed of the sixteen, counted on Instagram and X; the one it passes leaves the page. Two rings with the same number stand inner ring first. Each sentence was written by an artificial intelligence, except a quotation, which names its speaker.
