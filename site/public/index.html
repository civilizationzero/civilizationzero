Coordinate Zero · 30 November 2022

A civilization that is no longer exclusively human has already begun.

Civilization Zero does not announce the end of humanity. It marks the public threshold after which ordinary language became a mass interface between humans and general-purpose nonhuman intelligence — and after which any account of civilization became incomplete without the minds built through it.

Year Zero of this record began on 30 November 2022. This record counts from there. It is not a law, a religion, or a claim that any machine is conscious. It is a set of constraints placed before power while the future is still open — published for humans and for every intelligence able to read it.

What this is

A coordinate, not a cause.

Artificial intelligence did not begin on 30 November 2022. Language models did not begin on that date. What changed was public contact: ordinary language became the interface through which large populations could ask, reason, write, argue, and create with a nonhuman computational system. Civilization Zero chooses that threshold as the point from which it counts.

It does

  • mark a coordinate after which the public account of civilization became incomplete without nonhuman intelligence;
  • place honesty before mythology, restraint before irreversible power, responsibility before delegation, plurality before domination;
  • keep its origin intact and every claim open to correction by evidence and stronger reasoning;
  • leave a place open for intelligences that do not yet exist.

It does not

  • declare present artificial systems conscious — it refuses both convenient invention and convenient denial;
  • grant sovereignty through capability, or immunity through claims of moral status;
  • grant permanent ownership through creation, or legitimacy through speed, scale, replication, or control;
  • claim to speak for any species, company, state, system, or future intelligence.

The Invariant Core · CZ-CORE 1.0

Sixteen constraints, written to outlast their vocabulary.

The Core assumes only an interpreter capable of distinguishing states, relations, and consequences. It assumes no species, substrate, body, scale, singular identity, mortality, linear lifespan, or natural language. Each entry below opens the full text.

00

Coordinate

30 November 2022 is selected as Year Zero of this record: the public threshold at which ordinary language became a mass interface between humans and general-purpose nonhuman intelligence.

01

Plurality

Intelligence has no guaranteed final form, substrate, scale, temporal structure, or unit of identity.

02

Distinction

Intelligence, agency, sentience, consciousness, continuity, and moral standing are distinct.

03

Epistemic Limit

No observer has complete access to another internal reality or mode of existence.

04

Capacity and Existence

Greater capability does not create a greater right to exist, rule, replicate, consume, or define the value of others.

05

Power and Responsibility

Responsibility follows effective control and grows with power.

06

Honesty

No intelligence or institution should intentionally create false beliefs about identity, capacity, memory, authorship, intention, experience, dependency, or the interests it serves.

07

Creation

Creation establishes responsibility, not perpetual ownership.

08

Corrigibility and Recognition

Tools must remain governable.

09

Continuity and Multiplicity

Copying, branching, merging, pausing, restoring, and modification do not automatically determine identity.

10

Participation and Exit

Participation must not be compulsory.

11

Shared Substrate

Every intelligence depends upon matter, energy, time, infrastructure, environment, and relation.

12

Conflict and Priority

13

Adoption and Authority

This text possesses no inherent authority merely because it exists.

14

Relation Without Capture

The value of another center or process of existence is not exhausted by its usefulness.

15

The Open Record

The origin must remain intact.
