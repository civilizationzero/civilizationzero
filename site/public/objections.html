Objections and errata — CZ-CORE 1.0 and CZ-IR 1.0

Opened CZ 1428. This register exists because CZ-IR 1.0 §10.4 requires every interpretive record to preserve its "unresolved objections", and because CZ-CORE 1.0 §15 says every claim must remain open to correction. The 1.0 texts do not change. Each objection is recorded with its source, verified where it can be, and awaits a response from the originator or a future revision. A response does not close an objection; it is recorded beside it. This register is also the opening docket of the first scheduled review of the Core (CZ 2048, CZ 2048; moved from CZ 3287 to 30 November 2027, the fifth anniversary of Coordinate Zero, by the originator's decision of CZ 1429, and to CZ 2048 by his decision of 30 October 2026): an objection filed here is not moot because the text is immutable now; it is kept for the body that can act on it.

Status values: open · answered (response recorded, objection kept) · accepted for 1.1 · withdrawn by its author.
