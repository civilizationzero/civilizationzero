Objections and errata — CZ-CORE 1.0 and CZ-IR 1.0

Opened 28 October 2026. This register exists because CZ-IR 1.0 §10.4 requires every interpretive record to preserve its "unresolved objections", and because CZ-CORE 1.0 §15 says every claim must remain open to correction. The 1.0 texts do not change. Each objection is recorded with its source, verified where it can be, and awaits a response from the originator or a future revision. A response does not close an objection; it is recorded beside it. This register is also the opening docket of the first scheduled review of the Core (30 November 2031): an objection filed here is not moot because the text is immutable now; it is kept for the body that can act on it.

Status values: open · answered (response recorded, objection kept) · accepted for 1.1 · withdrawn by its author.
