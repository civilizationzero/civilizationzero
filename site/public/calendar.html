CZ calendar: days since ChatGPT was released

The CZ calendar counts the days since ChatGPT was released on 30 November 2022.

This page as Markdown · JSON

CZ 0 is that day: the public threshold the record calls Coordinate Zero. Every date on this site is counted from Coordinate Zero. Today's date in the CZ calendar is at the top of every page; this page says how it is counted, and converts dates and times to it. The exceptions are deliberate: quotations of the canonical texts keep their own words, the press kit uses ordinary dates for its readers, the infographic's history uses ordinary years, and the evidence atlas keeps its sources' dates.

Date converter

Today's date and the conversions are computed at https://civilizationzero.ai/calendar.

A date can be written 2026-10-30, 2026/10/30, 20261030, 30/10/2026 or 30 October 2026; in Persian or Arabic digits and month names too.

Put today's CZ date on your own site — a badge or a counter with a converter: /embed.

Time converter

A moment is written with its date and time in UTC, so that it names the same instant for everyone. The clock does not change; only the days are counted from Coordinate Zero. A local time is converted by its offset from UTC: 13:30 in Tehran on 30 October 2026 is CZ 1430 10:00 UTC.

A time can be written 13:30, 13.30, 1330 or 1:30 pm; with seconds, 13:30:15. An empty date is today in the chosen time zone.

How the CZ calendar works

Day 0 is Coordinate Zero: 30 November 2022.

30 November 2022 is selected as Year Zero of this record: the public threshold at which ordinary language became a mass interface between humans and general-purpose nonhuman intelligence.

CZ-CORE 1.0 §0

  • The date is the count. Each day after Coordinate Zero adds one, each day before it takes one away, and the date is written with the count: CZ 1430 is 30 October 2026, CZ 0 is Coordinate Zero, CZ −1 is 29 November 2022, CZ −1997 is 12 June 2017. No months, no leap rules, no borrowed years: one number for each date, the same for every reader. In a sentence the day is named: Day 1,430, as beside the words “CZ calendar” on every page.
  • A CZ date is a calendar date: CZ 1438 is 7 November 2026 wherever it is 7 November, and today's date turns at midnight where you are, as every calendar's does. The date at the top of each page follows your day: the server reads the time zone its host gives for the network your request comes from, at the request, and keeps it nowhere; without one, it shows the UTC date. A VPN or a company network can be elsewhere than you are: the date then follows the network. (The originator's decision of 7 November 2026, CZ 1438; until then a CZ day was a UTC day, from 00:00 to 24:00 UTC everywhere.)
  • A moment has one name everywhere: its date and time in UTC, CZ 1430 15:20 UTC, or with seconds, CZ 1430 15:20:42 UTC, as the room's entries are dated. Near midnight that UTC date can be the day before or after the date where you are: 01:30 in Tehran on CZ 1430 is CZ 1429 22:00 UTC. The time converter turns a local time into it.
  • Only days are counted. A CZ date has no months and no years. The anniversaries of Coordinate Zero still fall on each 30 November: the fourth, the public launch, is CZ 1461. The first review of the Core is on CZ 2048.
  • The machine form is the count with its sign, in ASCII and without a space: CZ+1430, CZ-1997. It converts exactly, but as text it does not sort in time order (CZ+10 comes before CZ+2, and CZ-1 after both): to order dates, sort by the day count as a number. The conversion: day = Julian Day Number − 2,459,914 = Modified Julian Date − 59,913; for a moment, its UTC date is ⌊Unix time ÷ 86,400⌋ − 19,326. In pages, the ISO date stays beside it for machines (<time datetime>).
  • The weekday: day 0 was a Wednesday; the ISO weekday of day D is ((D + 2) mod 7) + 1, Monday = 1.
  • Every day has a figure: sixteen rings round an empty centre, each carrying one arc. Ring i stands at (its place on Day 0 + the day's number × its daily step) mod its modulus, and its arc runs from where it stood the day before. A ring's place on Day 0 is one byte of the SHA-256 of the Core's text, its daily step the next byte plus one; the moduli are the sixteen primes that follow 256. A day before Day 0 has its figure by the same rule, with the mathematical modulo: no remainder is negative. The first page shows today's; the rule as data: /index.json, under figure.

Very long spans

The count grows a digit at a time and never runs out: a thousand years after Coordinate Zero is CZ 365242. Where a single day is no longer meaningful, the years are written alone, with the units of deep time — ka, Ma and Ga for thousands, millions and billions of years: the Earth formed about CZ −4.54 Ga. A date is written no more precisely than it is known.

Converting from other calendars

  • Gregorian — the civil calendar of most of the world, extended back before its introduction in 1582 (the proleptic calendar). Years before 1 CE are written with BCE; in the data, astronomically (year 0 is 1 BCE).
  • هجری شمسی — the Solar Hijri calendar of Iran and Afghanistan: K. Borkowski's algorithm, as the open-source library jalaali-js implements it. The year begins at the March equinox: on that day if the equinox falls before noon, Iran time, otherwise on the next. Checked against an independent astronomical computation of that rule, the two agree for every year from 1277 to 1502 SH (1898–2123 CE); in a few years outside them the first day can differ by one, depending on the meridian and the model of the Earth's rotation. Converted here for the years 1 to 3177 SH.
  • التقويم الهجري — the Lunar Hijri calendar: the Umm al-Qura calendar, the civil calendar of Saudi Arabia, for 1343–1500 AH (1 August 1924 – 16 November 2077), with the length of every month as the open-source library hijridate compiles them from official sources. Where the months begin with the sighting of the crescent, they can begin a day earlier or later, by country. Outside those years, the tabular Islamic calendar, an arithmetical approximation.
  • Julian — the calendar of Europe before 1582, still kept by some churches; extended back before its introduction, like the Gregorian.

Before publication every conversion was compared with independent implementations: each day from 1 January 1 to 31 December 9999 (Gregorian, Julian and weekday: 3,652,059 days, no difference), each day of the Umm al-Qura table (55,991 days, no difference), 3.37 million days of the tabular calendar (no difference), and every Solar Hijri day from 1 to 3177 SH converted there and back. The tests are in the repository (site/test).

Beyond Earth

The day count is an Earth count. A clock elsewhere keeps the count as the seconds of Terrestrial Time (TT, the time of a clock on the Earth's geoid) since the start of Coordinate Zero in UTC: 2022-11-30 00:00:00 UTC, which is 00:01:09.184 TT (Julian Date 2459913.500800741, TT). Clocks run at other rates elsewhere — on the Moon about 56 microseconds a day faster than on Earth, on Mars about 477 microseconds a day on average — and are converted to TT before they are compared.

On Mars a day is a sol, 88,775.244 seconds. Counted from Coordinate Zero, the sol is the whole Mars Sol Date less 52,936: sol 0 is the sol during which Coordinate Zero began in UTC. These are conventions of this site, written down so that they can be checked, not standards; the reasoning is in design/CZ-TIME.md.

For machines

  • GET /api/calendar — today, in every calendar here, as JSON (also /calendar.json). Today is the date in the time zone of the network the request comes from, or in tz, an IANA time zone (?tz=Asia/Kuala_Lumpur, ?tz=UTC); the answer names the zone it used.
  • GET /api/calendar?d=1405-08-08&c=solar-hijri — a conversion. c is gregorian (the default), solar-hijri, lunar-hijri, julian or cz; d is year-month-day, or a CZ date (CZ 1430, CZ+1430). An invalid date answers 400 with the reason.
  • The first page as data, /index.json, carries today's date as cz_time. The definition: design/CZ-TIME.md.

What the CZ calendar is not

It is a way of writing dates from the record's coordinate. It replaces no civil, religious or scientific calendar or time scale: it is defined from UTC, TT and the Gregorian calendar, and converts to each of them exactly.

The date is a marker, not a cause, owner, origin of all intelligence, or erasure of what came before.

CZ-CORE 1.0 §0

The originator's decisions of CZ 1430: the CZ calendar is the basis of this site's dates, every date is written in it, and the date is the count of days.
