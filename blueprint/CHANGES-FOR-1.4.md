| 8 | **The verifier.** `site/verify/verify.py`, published at `/archive/verify.py` and inside every bundle from 1.0.1 (`verify/verify.py`): Python 3.8+, standard library only, no network unless asked. It checks the key against the fingerprint printed in it, every signature (pure-Python Ed25519 per RFC 8032, cofactorless as OpenSSL), every hash in the release manifest, the translations' mapping by hash, each OpenTimestamps proof's commitment and its path up to the Bitcoin block (printing the Merkle root to compare), and the discussion's daily chain (each day = SHA-256(previous hash + "\n" + lines); continuity; content hashes; signatures). Its limit is stated where it is published: a copy can be altered with the bundle it came with; the fingerprint must be compared with a copy obtained elsewhere. Tested against the RFC vectors, 750 differential cases against OpenSSL, the OpenTimestamps reference proofs, and day hashes produced by Postgres. | §8 self-containment: "no verification depends on a live service" — it also should not depend on trusting the publisher's own tools |
| 7.2, 8 | **The daily chain leaves the site.** `/archive/entries.json` indexes every hashed day. The public workflow `daily-archive` (01:20 and 07:20 UTC; the site's own job runs in the first hour of the day, as its host schedules it) copies each new day into the repository (`archive/discussion/{date}.json` snapshot, `{date}.log` = the exact hashed bytes, `{date}.log.ots` = the OpenTimestamps proof of the day's hash), after checking the whole chain with the verifier; `ots-upgrade` runs every six hours and upgrades every proof; `verify-live` runs the verifier against the live site daily. Each bundle states the last day it carries (`in_bundle_through`). Once the room has opened, its chain is kept, served and hashed whatever the room's state, and its entries stay readable at their addresses — the pages, the API, the moderation log and the archive (closing the room stops new entries, never the record); a site or mirror must serve every day from the first (2026-10-28) to the last one due — the verifier fails one that omits a day at either end (review findings T-1, T-1b). | §7.2 "both go into the next bundle"; a rewritten day must contradict copies the site does not hold |
